# Hours of Solomon

A personal web app for studying the Key of Solomon and the Lesser Key: the current planetary hour and what it is good for, the items and rites of the books with the next time each fits, and a codex of spirits, astrology, numbers and Kabbalah.

Everything is worked out on the device from your city and the date. Your name, birthday and progress stay in your own browser.

The site is built from source kept outside this repository; the files here are the built output served by GitHub Pages.
